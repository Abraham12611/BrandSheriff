Review: Northstar Atelier Meridian Carry-On

By Travel Daily

Northstar Atelier's tagline is "Move light. Arrive ready." and the Meridian Carry-On mostly lives up to it. We tested the $295 carry-on across two weeks in Europe.

The lightweight shell and smart interior pockets make it a strong pick for one-bag travelers. It is not the cheapest option, but the build quality justifies the price for frequent flyers.

Disclosure: Travel Daily purchased this product at retail price.
